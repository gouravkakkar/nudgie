Nine hours at a screen. Your eyes and back pay for it.

Nudgie is a tiny menu bar app that looks after the parts of you a desk job wears down — eyes, back, shoulders, water, legs. Five small nudges, timed to the hours you're actually at the Mac, so you finish the day with something left.

It never lands mid-sentence.

A health reminder you mute on day two is worth nothing. So Nudgie waits.

  • 📷
    Camera or mic in useAny app, any call. Nudgie only reads an "is this device busy" flag, so there is no permission prompt and no recording.
  • 🖥
    A browser or meeting app in frontSafari, Chrome, Arc, Firefox, Zoom, Teams, Slack, FaceTime, Webex, Discord. Edit the list.
  • ⏱
    Then a 30-second breatherReminders that came due wait, and each one waits at most once. A long call earns you one eye break afterwards, not three.

Counts real time, not clock time.

Timers only run while you're using the Mac. Lock the screen, close the lid, or walk away for five minutes and everything resets. That was your break.

Coming back from lunch never triggers a pile of stale reminders, and a delayed card never fires the moment you sit down.

Optional work hours, a pause menu, and "take a break now" for when you don't want to wait.

No health data, anywhere

No account, no analytics, no network calls. Nudgie keeps two numbers — breaks taken and snoozed, today and yesterday — in your Mac's user defaults, and throws the rest away.

Native, not a web page in a box

Written in Swift for macOS. No Electron, no background helpers, no permission prompts. Roughly a hundredth the size.

Install in a minute.

  1. Download Nudgie.app.zip, unzip it, and move Nudgie.app to Applications.
  2. macOS 15 or newer: open it, click Done on the "cannot verify" dialog, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway, then Open.
  3. macOS 14: right-click Nudgie.app → Open → Open.

Why the extra step? Nudgie isn't notarised yet (that needs a paid Apple developer account). You only do this once. If you prefer the terminal:

xattr -d com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Nudgie.app

Or build it yourself with Xcode 26: git clone https://github.com/gouravkakkar/nudgie.git && cd nudgie && make install

Screen time is the job now, and eye strain, a stiff neck and a forgotten water bottle come with it. A free alternative to LookAway (from $19) and Time Out's paid unlocks, and a native, lighter alternative to Stretchly.
